01Research Snapshot5 gaps

I scraped the apply page, the free-course funnel, and every piece of public press I could find. Then I mapped your funnel end to end and identified five concrete gaps that are leaking revenue right now.

Audience profile

Creators stuck $0-$10k/mo, predominantly male 22-35, US/UK/CA/AU/EU based, working a day job, allergic to being on camera, fatigued from 30+ gurus repeating the same thumbnail advice.

Five gaps I found

  • Gap 1. No nurture sequence after the free-course opt-in. Warm leads who do not buy the Whop upsell go cold immediately.
  • Gap 2. Zero paid ad layer. Your VSL converts. Meta ads against the free course would feed it predictably.
  • Gap 3. Thin social proof on the apply page. Names and dollar amounts, no photos, no quotes, no video clips. In a Stage 5 market with a public "scam allegations" video on Google, thin proof costs trust.
  • Gap 4. No retargeting pixel I could see. Apply-page traffic that does not convert is wasted.
  • Gap 5. No founder bio or FAQ on the apply page. The press footprint (Wedbush wire, 25B views, $110k/mo AdSense) never reaches the people watching the VSL.

Highest-ROI move

Gap 1. The post-opt-in nurture sequence is the single highest-leverage thing you could ship in the next 14 days. Everything else compounds slower.

02Campaign Angles4 angles

Four angles, each with a name, a headline, and a 2-3 sentence hook. Built to test against the same audience from different psychological entry points.

1. Trust Score Exploit (Mechanism angle)

YouTube is not random. There is a hidden score the algorithm assigns to every channel, and three students just used it to go from $0 to $10k/mo in under 8 weeks.

2. $5k to $35k in 11 Weeks (Proof-led)

Lewis went from $5k/mo to $35k/mo on YouTube without a single new piece of equipment, a new niche, or a new face on camera.

3. Operator, Not Creator (Identity angle)

The creators making $30k/mo on YouTube are not the most creative people on the platform. They are the most systematic.

4. The Channel That Runs Without You (Anti-burnout angle)

Adam works 9-to-5 with two kids. His YouTube channel made $10k last month and he filmed zero of the videos.

Script 3. Educational

05VSL Outline6 sections

A 12 to 18 minute VSL outline that can be expanded into a full 2,800 to 4,000 word script. Same Trust Score mechanism, restructured to handle the objections I saw on Reddit and YouTube comments about your offer.

The six sections

  • Hook (0:00-0:45). Bold claim, pattern interrupt, the one-sentence headline.
  • Problem / agitation (0:45-3:00). Stack five pains in the buyer's own language, then land the diagnosis.
  • Solution / mechanism (3:00-7:00). Name Trust Score, define the three signals, walk through Lewis's $5k to $35k case as the worked example.
  • Credibility (7:00-10:00). Personal, then student wall, then the scam-allegations preempt.
  • Offer overview (10:00-13:00). "Done being a creator" framing. Soft scarcity around the cohort cap.
  • CTA (13:00-14:30). One button. End on a frame, not a hard close.
