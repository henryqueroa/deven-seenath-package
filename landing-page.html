The Real Reason Your Channel Is Stuck

YouTube does not score videos. It scores channels. Every channel on the platform has a hidden number attached to it, and that number decides how much reach your next upload gets, regardless of how good the video is. We call it the Trust Score, and it is made of three signals you can operate on purpose.

01

Engagement Velocity

The slope of likes, comments and saves in the first 60 minutes after publish. Most creators chase the total. The algorithm watches the slope.

02

Retention Loops

Whether the second half of your video holds harder than the first half. This is what turns a 50k view video into a 5 million view video. Average view duration alone will not get you there.

03

Brand-Safe Delivery

Whether YouTube trusts your channel enough to place your video in front of advertisers' best customers. The hidden signal. Almost every channel under $10k/mo fails this without knowing it exists.
